Common questions

Does a sweep always mean I need a new liner?

No. A sweep removes soot and creosote. A liner quote is for a damaged, unlined, or wrongly sized flue. Ask to see the camera of the clay tiles first.

Can a cap or crown be the leak instead of the liner?

Yes. Water at the firebox is often a missing rain cap or a cracked crown. Ask which defect they photographed before you approve a full reline.

Is this the same checklist as who pulls a water-heater permit?

No. Permit Pulled is Denver tank-unit permit work. This page is Chicago masonry chimneys: sweep and inspect versus stainless liner.

Why do liner quotes jump into the thousands?

A listed stainless liner plus tile demolition, insulation, top plate, and cap is a different job than a sweep. Ask which of those lines is on the quote.
