Sweep quoted a liner. Ask for the camera of the flue tiles before you approve stainless.

Chicago chimney jobs mix a few-hundred-dollar sweep with a three-to-six-thousand-dollar liner. Homeowners keep getting liner numbers when the leak was a cap or crown. Use this list before you approve a reline.

  • Ask whether this visit is a sweep and inspection or a liner install.
  • Ask for camera photos of the clay tiles before a liner quote.
  • Ask if the leak is a missing cap or failed crown instead.
  • If they still want a liner, ask whether tile demo, insulation, top plate, and cap are in the number.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
